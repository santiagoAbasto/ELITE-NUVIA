# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ELITE Nuvia staff: SUPER_ADMIN users managing the public website and business operations, COORDINADOR users supervising agents and pipeline activity, and AGENTE users working assigned leads, agenda items, captaciones, and property records. They use the product during daily real estate operations, where speed, clarity, and role-aware access matter.

## Product Purpose

ELITE Nuvia combines a public luxury real estate website with a private admin panel for CMS and CRM work. The admin must let authorized staff edit public site content, manage properties, agents, captaciones, leads, agenda events, reports, media uploads, and PDF outputs while preserving the premium public brand experience.

## Brand Personality

Premium, precise, trustworthy. The public brand is cinematic and polished, using deep green, gold, cream, refined typography, and real estate imagery. The admin should feel like the operational cockpit behind that public experience: elegant, capable, and calm under dense information.

## Anti-references

Do not make the admin feel like a generic black SaaS dashboard, a plain form shell, or a disconnected back office unrelated to the public site. Avoid decorative purple-blue gradients, excessive glass effects, vague placeholder metrics, weak contrast, inactive-looking controls, and preview actions that do not reflect the public website.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the public brand present in the admin through restrained green and gold accents, typography, and content previews.
2. Make the primary workflow obvious on each screen: edit content, review pipeline, inspect property, save, preview, or publish.
3. Surface real business data from the CMS and CRM instead of filling space with decorative panels.
4. Preserve role clarity: SUPER_ADMIN, COORDINADOR, and AGENTE users should understand what they can see and change.
5. Treat preview as a first-class part of CMS editing, so staff can verify how public content will read before leaving the admin.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, visible keyboard focus states, clear disabled and loading states, readable Spanish labels, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential transitions.
